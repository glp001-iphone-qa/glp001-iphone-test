About these collections

Daily Nitnem groups seven reading destinations. The directory also contains Sukhmani Sahib and the two Salok collections. Some Nitnem readings come from Dasam Bani, and Rehras is a composite reading; the directory is not a measure of complete Guru Granth Sahib coverage.

Each reader retains its own source edition, documented differences and review status. Repeated passages within different compositions keep their existing place.
